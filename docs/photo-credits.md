# Photo credits

Photos on the site that are not ours. Each licence asks for credit; the credit line is shown under the photos on the
project's page (see `photoCredit` in `data/index.ts`).

The photos below are **cropped and colour-corrected** (`scripts/process-completed-photos.mjs`). The edited versions stay
under the same licence as the originals (CC BY-SA, share alike). The full-size originals are kept outside the site, in
`reference/photos-originals/` (not committed).
